Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child first visit the dentist?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that a child visit the dentist by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth erupting. Early visits help establish a "dental home" and allow us to monitor development.

Are dental X-rays safe for children?

Yes, dental X-rays are extremely safe. We use modern digital X-rays that emit significantly less radiation than traditional ones, and we always use lead aprons to ensure your child's absolute safety.

How do you handle a child who is scared of the dentist?

We specialize in behavior management techniques such as "Tell-Show-Do," positive reinforcement, and creating a fun, distracting environment. Our priority is to build trust and ensure the visit is completely anxiety-free.

Why repair baby teeth if they will fall out anyway?

Baby teeth are crucial for proper chewing, speaking, and holding space for permanent teeth. Untreated decay can cause severe pain, infection, and damage to the developing permanent teeth beneath them.
